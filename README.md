

# Accelerated Cybersecurity Posture Assessment




When conducting a cybersecurity posture assessment of a company using the NIST CSF 2.0, CIS Controls V8, and ISO/IEC 27001 frameworks, The aim is to evaluate the most critical aspects of the current security measures, identify key vulnerabilities, and suggest high-impact enhancements.


The procedure for conducting a Posture Assessment includes the following;

- Hold an initial briefing to outline project objectives, and distribute roles, and responsibilities.
- Familiarize the team with the essential elements of NIST CSF 2.0, CIS Controls V8, and ISO/IEC 27001.
- Prioritize areas of assessment based on preliminary risk indications and business criticality.
- Gather existing security policies, process documentation, and system configurations.
- Use automated tools to scan the network and systems for vulnerabilities.
- Conduct interviews with key IT staff to understand current security practices and issues.
- Map collected data against the standards and controls specified in NIST CSF, CIS Controls, and ISO 27001.
- Identify gaps in the current security posture relative to the frameworks.
- Highlight critical vulnerabilities and assess potential impacts on business operations.
- Compile findings into a structured report, detailing observed vulnerabilities, gaps in compliance, and areas for improvement.
- Develop prioritized recommendations for mitigating identified risks, aligning improvements with business goals and resource availability.
- Prepare a presentation summarizing key findings and recommendations.
- Present the assessment results to the cybersecurity department and other relevant stakeholders.
- A detailed assessment report including compliance status with NIST CSF, CIS Controls, and ISO 27001, vulnerabilities found, and prioritized recommendations.

  
Next Steps & Deliverables; 
When the assessment has be conducted, some concrete next steps are to prioritize the high-risk gaps in patch management and third-party vendor risk scoring prior to the upcoming audit cycle.
