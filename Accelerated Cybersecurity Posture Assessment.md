# Accelerated Cybersecurity Posture Assessment

> Audit and compliance: CIS Controls v8 gap assessment and a prioritized 60/90-day remediation roadmap.

## Objective

Establish an evidence-based view of cybersecurity maturity and deliver a prioritized remediation roadmap within a compressed timeline and limited resources.

## Execution

- I defined the engagement objectives, scope, roles and timeline.
- I mapped policies, procedures and system configurations to CIS Controls v8 and tested them against what was actually in place, not only what was documented.
- I interviewed IT and security staff to validate practice and surface operational gaps that documents missed.
- I prioritized assessment areas by risk severity and business criticality, then rated each gap by likelihood and business impact.
- I sequenced remediation by risk reduction versus effort, factoring in budget and staffing constraints.

## Business outcomes

- Identified control gaps, including critical and high-risk items needing immediate action, and increased baseline maturity by **30%**.
- Delivered a **60/90-day roadmap** that leadership could fund and assign, with the highest-risk items first and owners named.

## Skills demonstrated

- Control gap assessment
- Stakeholder interviews and evidence validation
- Risk-based prioritization
- Remediation roadmapping
